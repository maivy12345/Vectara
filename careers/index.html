Career tracks

Your trajectory

A clear path from specialist to programme lead.

Progression is based on what you deliver and the responsibility you take, not on time served.

  1. L1

    Specialist

    Own a discipline model or dataset and deliver it to standard.

  2. L2

    Senior Specialist

    Run coordination for a package and mentor colleagues.

  3. L3

    Lead

    Lead the information workstream for a whole project.

  4. L4

    Programme Lead

    Set information strategy across programmes and advise clients.
