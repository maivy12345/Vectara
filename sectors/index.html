Sectors

The same information discipline across different asset types.

We work across infrastructure, industrial development, buildings and operations — bringing the same independent, model-driven approach to each.

01

Infrastructure & utilities

Typical challenge

Underground utility networks are where spatial clashes are most frequent and costly.

How we help

We federate services and run disciplined clash detection so conflicts are resolved in the model before they are dug up on site.

02

Industrial & site development

Typical challenge

Earthworks and platform development need honest volumes and as-built records.

How we help

We process reality capture into volumetric and as-built data, so progress and the final record are measured, not estimated.

03

Buildings & mixed-use

Typical challenge

Multi-discipline buildings demand coordination and structured asset-data handover.

How we help

We coordinate disciplines in one model and hand over structured asset data the operator can use.

04

Asset owners & operators

Typical challenge

Owners need a digital record they can operate and maintain from.

How we help

We deliver a verified digital twin — model, survey and asset data — built for the operating life, not just construction.
