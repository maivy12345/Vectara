Our approach

Digital, spatial and model-driven.

Vectara works to ISO 19650 under the project BIM Execution Plan. Information is produced, coordinated and governed through a controlled Common Data Environment, on a fixed coordination cadence, with reality from site fed back into the model.

Five-step method

Govern first. Coordinate continuously. Verify at handover.

One closed loop, not a straight line. What we capture on site at step five flows back into the model, so the record stays a measurement — never a reconstruction from memory.

  1. 01Govern

    BEP & CDE set-up

    We establish naming, states, access and the information delivery plan before modelling begins.

    Standards imposed after the fact are standards ignored — so the plan is agreed with all parties up front.

    • BIM Execution Plan
    • CDE states
    • Naming
    • Access
  2. 02Model

    Model & federate

    Discipline models are authored and federated to the agreed Level of Information Need.

    Each model is checked for health, naming, warnings and detail before federation — problems are caught before they multiply.

    • LOIN
    • Model health
    • Federation
  3. 03Coordinate

    Coordinate & clash

    The federated model is checked on a fixed cycle; clashes are classified, assigned and tracked to closure before the affected work proceeds.

    A defined clash matrix directs effort to the discipline pairs where conflicts actually occur.

    • Fixed cycle
    • Clash matrix
    • Tracked to closure
  4. 04Measure

    Quantify & report

    5D quantities and model-health reports are produced each cycle to support measurement and decisions.

    Quantities are reconciled against design intent, so divergence is raised as a question — not discovered as a surprise.

    • 5D quantities
    • Health reports
    • Reconciliation
  5. 05Verify

    Capture & hand over

    Reality capture from site updates the model; at completion we verify and hand over the digital twin and structured asset data.

    A regular feedback cycle keeps as-built information current and makes the final record a measurement.

    • Reality capture
    • Digital twin
    • Asset data

Three principles

The method, in three moves.

WIPSharedPublishedArchived

01 — Govern first

Information has a state before it has a shape.

Every container moves through controlled CDE states, with naming and access agreed before the first element is modelled.

02 — Coordinate continuously

A fixed cadence makes progress visible.

Clashes are classified, assigned and closed on a cycle, with effort focused where the clash matrix says conflicts actually occur.

03 — Verify at handover

The as-built is measured, not remembered.

Point-cloud capture from site is checked against the model, so the digital twin you receive matches the asset you own.
