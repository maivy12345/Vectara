Privacy Policy

How Vectara Digital Infrastructure Limited collects, uses and protects personal data.

Last updated8 October 2026Contact us →

Overview

Vectara Digital Infrastructure Limited is committed to protecting your personal data and complying with the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486). This policy explains what we collect through vectaragroup.com, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights.

Personal data we collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, company, email address, telephone number and message. We may also collect limited technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and access time through cookies and analytics.

Purposes

We use personal data to respond to enquiries, provide and improve services, operate and secure the website, send requested information and comply with legal obligations.

Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We may share it with service providers that operate our website and communications under confidentiality and data-protection obligations, and with authorities where required by law.

Transfer, storage and retention

Data may be processed in Hong Kong or other jurisdictions with appropriate safeguards. Enquiry records are retained for up to 24 months after last contact unless a relationship continues; analytics data follows provider settings, typically 14 months.

Security and your rights

We use access controls, encryption in transit and secure hosting. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion, or withdraw consent, by contacting services@vectaragroup.com.
