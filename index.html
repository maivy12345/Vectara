FAQ

Clear answers, before work begins.

Scope, standards, ownership and delivery — answered directly.

Do you operate survey equipment on site?

No. Site capture, including laser scanning and UAV work, is performed by others; we process that data and govern the information from our Hong Kong office.

What standard do you work to?

ISO 19650-1/-2 under the project BIM Execution Plan, using Autodesk Revit or Civil 3D and Navisworks.

Who owns the CDE and project data?

The client owns the Common Data Environment, its accounts, licences and all data. We administer it under delegated authority.

What is a digital twin in practice?

A verified as-built model plus structured asset data the owner can operate and maintain from, kept aligned with reality.

Can you take over BIM coordination on a live project?

Yes. We can establish or take over the BEP, CDE and federated model and run coordination from there.

What is clash detection?

Automated checking of the combined model for geometric conflicts and clearance violations, so they are resolved before construction.

Do you produce quantities?

Yes. We produce 5D quantity take-off from the model, traceable to the elements the quantities came from.

Where is project data hosted?

On the client-owned CDE, with ISO 19650 information-management and security controls.

Where are you based?

Vectara Digital Infrastructure Limited is based at Unit 1411, 14/F, Cosco Tower, 183 Queen's Road Central, Sheung Wan, Hong Kong.

Do you work internationally or remotely?

Yes. Most work is delivered through digital collaboration, with clear deliverables agreed up front and a defined reporting cadence.

How are engagements structured and priced?

Each engagement has a written scope, deliverables and fee, typically on a man-month or deliverable basis.

Can you join a project that is already underway?

Yes. We regularly mobilise on live projects, establish a working basis quickly and refine from there rather than stopping the works.

How quickly can you mobilise?

Usually within the first reporting cycle. We establish a credible working basis and data feed before refining the detail.

Who will actually do the work?

Senior specialists. We are deliberately a small, hands-on team — the people who win the work are the people who deliver it.

How do you ensure your output is reliable?

Senior review of every deliverable, version control, recognised standards and, where independence is required, separation between reviewer and producer.

Who owns the deliverables and data?

You do. Paid deliverables and project data transfer to you, and we protect confidential information throughout.

What do you need from us to start?

A clear point of contact, access to the relevant project information, and agreement on scope and cadence. We define the data interfaces up front.

Do you sign a non-disclosure agreement?

Yes. We can work under your NDA or ours, and we treat project information as confidential by default.

How do you report and communicate?

Through a single point of contact on a fixed cadence: a regular working cycle plus a periodic formal report, with prompt escalation of cost, programme, quality or risk issues.

How do we start working with you?

Email services@vectaragroup.com or use the contact form. We will arrange a short call to understand the project and propose a way forward.
