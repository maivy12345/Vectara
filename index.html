05 / CLARITY

Common questions

Practical answers for organisations planning a better digital delivery approach.

What is the difference between BIM and a digital twin?+

BIM provides structured information and models across an asset lifecycle. A digital twin connects selected asset information with operational or real-time data to support defined decisions and actions. Not every BIM model needs to become a digital twin.

When should information requirements be defined?+

As early as possible—before appointments and delivery plans are fixed. Clear requirements allow teams to price, plan, produce and verify information consistently.

Can you work with an existing project team and CDE?+

Yes. The approach is technology-neutral. We can review current workflows, identify gaps and strengthen governance without replacing tools that already serve the project well.

Do you support both projects and operational assets?+

Yes. Engagements can start at briefing, design, construction, handover or operation. The scope is shaped around the information decisions and risks at that stage.
