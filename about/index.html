Our purpose

From drawings and survey data to a living digital twin.

We deliver BIM coordination, digital twins, 5D quantities and Common Data Environment administration for infrastructure and built-environment programmes.

Our specialists work to ISO 19650 using Autodesk Revit and Civil 3D, Navisworks for coordination, and point-cloud processing tools for reality capture. We model, federate, detect clashes, extract quantities and govern project information on platforms owned and licensed by the client.

We are an information and modelling service. We process and govern data captured by others and provided by the client; we do not operate physical survey equipment on site. That boundary keeps responsibilities clear and the data trail auditable.
