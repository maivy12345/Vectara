Service

Common Data Environment administration

We govern the project's information so the right people have the right data.

The challenge

Find the problem while it is still cheap to solve.

When project information lives in a dozen inboxes and shared drives, there is no single version of the truth. A governed Common Data Environment fixes that.

Our approach

A repeatable, auditable delivery cycle.

We administer the client-owned CDE: folder structure, states, naming and access, plus the workflow that moves information from Work-in-Progress to Shared to Published to Archived. Each transition is checked and fully auditable.

What we do and deliver

  • CDE set-up and administration
  • Information-state workflow control
  • Transmittals and delivery management
  • Security, versioning and a full audit trail
  • Periodic data-health and model-health reporting

Standards

Working basis

ISO 19650 information management on client-owned platforms and licences.

Typical engagement

Delivery rhythm

Administered under delegated authority on the client's CDE throughout the project.

Outcome

A controlled, auditable single source of truth for project information.

Who it is for

Teams that need one controlled source of truth.

Owners, developers and contractors delivering infrastructure, utilities and other complex assets — from day one or as an independent review of work already underway.
