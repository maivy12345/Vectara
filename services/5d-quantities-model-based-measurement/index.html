Service

5D quantities & model-based measurement

We extract quantities straight from the model for faster, traceable measurement.

The challenge

Find the problem while it is still cheap to solve.

Measuring quantities by hand from drawings is slow, error-prone and difficult to audit. When the model carries the geometry, quantities can be extracted quickly and traced to the element they came from.

Our approach

A repeatable, auditable delivery cycle.

We extract 5D quantities directly from the federated model, link them to elements for traceability, and report them as the model develops. We also check model quantities against design intent to catch divergence early.

What we do and deliver

  • Automated quantity take-off from the federated model
  • Quantities linked to source elements for audit
  • Support for measurement, reconciliation and valuation
  • Periodic reporting as the model develops
  • Variance checks against design intent

Standards

Working basis

Model-based measurement traceable to source elements.

Typical engagement

Delivery rhythm

Quantities produced each cycle or on demand as the model matures.

Outcome

Quantities that are fast to produce, consistent and traceable to their source.

Who it is for

Teams that need quantities they can trace and defend.

Owners, developers and contractors delivering infrastructure, utilities and other complex assets — from day one or as an independent review of work already underway.
