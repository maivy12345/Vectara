Good governance makes the right information available at the right decision point.

It sets proportionate rules for creation, review, approval, security, exchange, retention and change—without creating bureaucracy for its own sake.

Control areaWhat good looks like
RequirementsInformation is requested because it supports a defined organisational, asset or project decision.
AccountabilityEach information container, attribute and approval has a named owner and clear responsibility.
ClassificationConsistent identifiers, status, revision and classification make information searchable and comparable.
QualityAcceptance criteria are machine-checkable where practical and reviewed at planned gates.
SecurityAccess, sensitivity and sharing are controlled according to risk and legitimate need.
LifecycleRetention, updates, archiving and disposal are designed beyond project handover.
