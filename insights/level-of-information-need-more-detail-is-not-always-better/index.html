Level of Information Need: more detail is not always better

  • Model standards
  • 2 min read
TopicModel standards
Reading time2 minutes
Share
LinkedInEmail
All insights ←

A common BIM mistake is modelling everything to the highest level of detail, as if more geometry were always better. It is not. Excess detail slows the model, inflates files, and buries the information that matters under information that does not.

ISO 19650 reframes the question around the Level of Information Need: what information is actually required, by whom, for which decision, at each stage. A model element needs enough detail to serve its purpose — and no more.

Getting this right is both a performance and a cost decision. Right-sized models are faster to coordinate, cheaper to produce, and easier to hand over.

We define the Level of Information Need up front in the BEP, so the model carries the information the project needs — not the information that merely looks impressive.
