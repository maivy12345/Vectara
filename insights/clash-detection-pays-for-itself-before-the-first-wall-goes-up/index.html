Two pipes cannot occupy the same space, but on a 2D drawing set they often appear to. Federated BIM puts every discipline’s model in one coordinate space and lets software find those conflicts automatically — hard clashes where geometry overlaps, soft clashes where clearances are violated.

The value is timing. A clash resolved in the model costs a coordinator’s afternoon. The same clash discovered on site costs a stoppage, a variation, and a delay to everything downstream. Multiply by the hundreds of conflicts a complex services route contains.

Effective clash detection is a managed process, not a one-off report: a fixed cycle, classification, ownership, and tracking to closure. A clash found but not closed is just a future problem with a timestamp.

We coordinate around the model on a fixed rhythm and only sign off a zone when its clashes are genuinely closed.
