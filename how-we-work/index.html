Working with Vectara

A clear scope, a fixed rhythm and accountable delivery.

Every engagement is different, but the way we work is consistent: clear deliverables, documented governance and a steady reporting cadence.

01

How an engagement starts

We begin with a short conversation to understand the project, its stage and where the pressure is. We then agree a clear scope, deliverables and timeline in writing.

02

Deliverables & cadence

Every deliverable has an owner, due date and acceptance step. A fixed working cycle and periodic formal report keep progress visible and accountable.

03

Governance & communication

We coordinate through a single client contact, document decisions and escalate cost, programme, quality or risk promptly.

04

Independence

We remain separate from the parties whose work we measure, review or model, making our output credible to owners, lenders and auditors.

05

Data & intellectual property

Project data and paid deliverables belong to the client. We protect confidential information and apply appropriate security throughout.

06

Fees

Engagements are scoped and priced in writing, typically on a man-month or deliverable basis.

A typical engagement rhythm

Consistency catches risk while it is still small.

After mobilisation, we establish the working basis and data interfaces in the first cycle, then settle into a fixed cadence: a regular working cycle with your team and a periodic formal report that consolidates the period and recommends actions.

As work approaches completion, the focus shifts to close-out: finalising the record, handing over deliverables and intellectual property, and supporting an orderly transfer to your team.
